Fly Or Not

Privacy Policy

Application: Fly Or Not (com.mouse.flynot) Effective Date: October 20, 2026
Summary: Fly Or Not is designed with your privacy in mind. The app operates 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data, location, or device identifiers. There are no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking, and no background services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android mobile application Fly Or Not ("the Game", "we", "our") handles user information. We believe in complete transparency and are committed to ensuring a safe, ad-free, and enjoyable gaming experience for users of all ages.

2. Information Collection and Usage

Fly Or Not does not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).

The game functions entirely offline on your Android device:

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, provide an email address, or log in to play.
  • Local Gameplay Data Only: Player nicknames entered for the high score table, score history, and audio settings (sound and music toggles) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's local SharedPreferences storage.
  • No Network Transmission: High scores, nicknames, gameplay progress, and settings are never sent across the internet to any external server, cloud database, or third party.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The game contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect, track, or share user data:

  • Zero Advertisements: Fly Or Not contains no advertisements. All ad networks (such as AdMob, StartApp, etc.) have been completely removed.
  • Zero Trackers or Analytics: We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or any other tracking tools to monitor user behavior or device parameters.
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: Because no data is collected, no information is ever shared with, sold to, or accessible by advertisers or third parties.

4. Device Permissions

Fly Or Not requires no sensitive permissions to install and operate:

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Location access (neither GPS nor network-based).
  • No Contacts, Phone, or SMS access.
  • No Storage permissions (no reading or writing external shared files).
  • No Internet access permission (android.permission.INTERNET is not requested).

5. Children's Privacy and Google Play Families Policy

Fly Or Not is a family-friendly casual game suitable for players of all ages, including children:

  • The game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable).
  • Parents and guardians can have peace of mind knowing that children can play safely without being tracked, profiled, or exposed to advertisements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored game information (such as local high scores and audio preferences) resides strictly in your device's private sandbox storage:

  • You can clear all local game data at any time via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Fly Or Not > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the game permanently deletes all locally stored data.

7. Security

Because Fly Or Not does not collect or transmit information over the internet, your gameplay data cannot be intercepted, leaked, or accessed remotely. Data safety is guaranteed by the Android operating system's application sandbox security model.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
